About Me

I’m an AI/ML Engineer focused on designing and building production-grade AI systems, with experience across Generative AI, Agentic AI, Conversational AI, Speech, NLP, Computer Vision, and multimodal systems. My work goes beyond integrating model APIs — I engineer the systems around them, including retrieval pipelines, agent orchestration, inference optimization, real-time communication, reliability, and scalable deployment.

I also focus heavily on performance engineering, including GPU utilization, multi-GPU scheduling, inference latency, memory optimization, and high-throughput AI pipelines.

My engineering approach is centered around taking AI systems from research to production — making them reliable, scalable, observable, and maintainable. I enjoy solving the difficult engineering problems behind AI products, whether that means reducing latency, improving inference efficiency, designing robust LLM infrastructure, or building intelligent systems that can operate reliably at scale.
